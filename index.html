City of Atlanta short-term rental rules (read before you hire anyone)

Inside City of Atlanta limits, short-term rentals require a Short-Term Rental License (STRL) under Ordinance 20-O-1656. As summarized on the City’s official page (checked 2026-10-30):

  • An owner or long-term tenant may license their primary residence plus one additional dwelling unit — up to two properties.
  • The STRL fee is listed as $150; issuance takes up to 10 business days; licenses renew annually.
  • The STRL number must appear on every online listing, and an STR agent must be designated.
  • A short-term rental is a stay of 30 consecutive days or fewer.
  • Operating without an STRL can bring citations and a one-year wait before a license application is accepted.

Official source: City of Atlanta — Short-Term Rental (Department of City Planning). Questions go to STR@atlantaga.gov. Rules change; check the City page for the current ordinance and amendments. Suburbs such as Decatur, Sandy Springs, Marietta, and unincorporated Cobb, DeKalb, Fulton, and Gwinnett counties have their own rules, and HOAs may prohibit STRs entirely.

How to compare Atlanta STR managers

  1. Compare the fee base, not just the percentage. Some charge on rental revenue only; others include cleaning fees in the base.
  2. Full-service vs hybrid. A 10% hybrid plan where you hire and pay cleaners is not comparable to a 20% full-service plan.
  3. Ask about one-time fees — onboarding, photography, furnishing, smart locks.
  4. Read the exit terms — notice period, cancellation fees, who owns the listing and reviews.
  5. Confirm licensing help. Inside Atlanta city limits, ask who files and renews the STRL and who serves as the STR agent.

Red flags

  • Guaranteed revenue projections with no comparable listings behind them.
  • Pitches that ignore the City of Atlanta primary-residence rule for a non-owner-occupied investment property.
  • Unclear fee base or add-on fees that aren’t in the written agreement.
  • Long lock-in terms or penalties to take your listing back.

FAQ

What do Airbnb property managers charge in Atlanta?

Among the companies listed here, published management fees range from about 10% (hybrid plans, or the low end of a quoted range) to 25–30% (premium full-service tiers). Exact rates depend on the property and service level — see each row and its source link.

Can I run an Airbnb on an investment property in the City of Atlanta?

The City’s ordinance allows a license for your primary residence plus one additional dwelling unit. Read the official City page linked above and ask a qualified attorney about your specific situation.
